# Historical equipment catalogue

`catalogue.csv` records real equipment dates separately from FS25 mod metadata. `installed` and `tested` remain `unknown` until the equipment is deliberately installed and exercised in-game.

## Research decisions

- Dates are earliest defensible availability or existence dates, not mod publication dates.
- The Pöttinger Pionier LW14 is dated 1963 by Pöttinger company-history material; the cited FS25 conversion's 14,000-litre capacity and 15 hp requirement fit the intended inherited role.
- Krone independently dates the first self-loading Alleslader to 1963. The FS25 Optimat conversion is therefore catalogued as a family-date candidate, not as proof of every configuration's date.
- The Welger RP150's 1978 date is explicitly an earliest confirmed existence date from a surviving January 1978 manual, not a claimed launch date.
- The Amazone D8-25 Super was not added because the located ModHub listing is FS22, not FS25.

## Deferred or rejected candidates

The following remain outside the catalogue pending configuration/fidelity inspection: Fiat 640 standalone; Fiat 420–640 combined pack; Landini 10000/13000; Landini Legend; CLAAS Dominator 88/98/108 SL; and any fantasy-power, huge-capacity, reskinned or otherwise misleading equipment.
